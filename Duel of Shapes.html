LORE

One day, two shapelings had their own points to prove – thus, they decided upon a duel.
The better shapes summoner wins.

Shapelings types:
Circleflash – His special summon's ability is speed.
Mischief Triangle – Can summon twice as many shapes in a single special attack (unless obstructed).

Possible areas to choose as a Field of Honour:
Mistwood – A pale, quiet forest shrouded in morning mist.
Sunscar – A dry, sun-baked expanse where the earth has turned gold.
Tideglass – A strange coastal arena surrounded by bright turquoise waters.
Emberfall – A scorched battlefield beneath the fading glow of a dying fire.

RULES

Characters move using WASD and arrow keys. They attack with Z/X and N/M, respectively.

Each summon type – lesser and greater – has their own summoning cooldown. On top of that,
after a certain number of summons, a shapeling has to wait a bit of time for their spent mana to replenish.

After opposing summons clash, they might get redirected onto nearby paths, push through or get dispelled.

The last shapeling standing wins.
